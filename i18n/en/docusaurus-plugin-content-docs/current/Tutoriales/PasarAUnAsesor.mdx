# Hand the conversation to a person

In Restaurante, `hablar_con_asesor` does not return to Start. The node transition is **Transfer to a human agent**: the bot gives notice and the conversation enters the queue on that same turn.

## The node

**Flows** → open **Restaurante** → the `hablar_con_asesor` node.

Under **Conversation**:

- **Intent:** `hablar_con_asesor`.
- **Response:** the notice. In the template it is "I'll connect you with someone on the team".
- **Transition:** **Transfer to a human agent**.

Under **Functionality**, **Ends a capture in progress** is on. If the person says it in the middle of an order, the bot stops asking and hands the conversation over.

![hablar_con_asesor: the message and the transfer](/img/diseno/asesor.png)

Mark only this intent. If the same transition is on `saludo`, the bot hands off even when the person says hello.

The notice text can also be changed under **Tools → Transfers**, in **Transfer message**. If the tree has no configuration, it still works, with the default notice.

## Try the notice

Console, **Restaurante** flow. Type `quiero hablar con un asesor`.

The bot sends the notice. In debug mode, after that notice there is no other bot bubble: you see who it reached. The bot stops replying while the conversation is with a person.

![The console in the Restaurante flow](/img/diseno/recorrido.png)

## The desk

**Operations → Help desk**. **Live inbox** separates Waiting, My chats, and All. **History** is what is already closed.

![Help desk: the live inbox](/img/operacion/mesa.png)

- **Waiting** are the ones nobody has claimed.
- When you choose one, the history is on the right and you write to the visitor.
- The operator claims, replies, closes, or returns the conversation to the bot.

The live conversation between operator and visitor works on the web widget. The detail of the queue, closing, and an external help desk is in [Help desk](/docs/Avanzado/MesaDeAyuda).
