# Getting Started

You sign in, plant a tree, and test it. The name of each piece is in the [terminology](/docs/Fundamentos/Terminologia).

## Sign in

![Sign-in: the workspace first, then the user](/img/intro/login.png)

1. Open <AppUrl>TreeFlow</AppUrl>.
2. Enter the **workspace** and press **Continue**.
3. Enter the user and the password, and press **Sign in**.

If you do not have an account yet, choose **Register your workspace**. It asks for the workspace name, the email, the administrator user, and the password. A six-digit code arrives; once you verify it, the account is created and you can download the credentials.

**Forgot your workspace?** and **Forgot your password?** use that same email.

In the header, once you are in, the workspace name is shown. Users, keys, and trees live there. Another customer is another workspace: you sign in again with its name.

## Plant the first tree

An empty workspace says **There are no trees in your forest**. The button is **Plant First Tree**.

If there are already trees, the menu has **Create a new bot**. In the list, **New Tree** opens the same screen, **Plant a New Tree**. You can also **Import Tree** from a backup.

![Plant a New Tree: create one or import a backup](/img/empezando/plantar.png)

The dialog has two parts.

**How you want to start.**

- **First time** turns on the tutorials and leaves the tree in [beginner mode](./ModoPrincipiante): the menu shows the canvas and hides intents, entities, messages, and captures. When you finish you can view the guide or skip it.
- **Experienced** loads sample data and leaves the full menu. In the tree selector that mode is called **Advanced**.

**The tree's details.**

- **Name**, required.
- **Description**, if you want one.
- **Agent industry**, required. Restaurant, hotel, clinic, and the rest bring a template of intents, entities, and a flow. **No industry** leaves the basic bot.
- **Base language**, the one used for training and for the interface.

**Plant Tree** creates it and opens the dashboard.

![The dashboard, with the tree already selected](/img/intro/panel.png)

At the top you choose the tree and the range. **Last Day** is the one selected when you open it.

| Card | What it counts |
|---|---|
| Conversations | How many there were in the range |
| Failure rate | What portion was not resolved |
| User sentiment | Whether sentiment analysis is on |
| Escalation to a human | How many were handed to a person |
| Flows, intents, entities | How many the tree has |
| Channels | How many are connected |
| Training | Whether it is up to date. Green means yes |

## Try it

1. The path is drawn in [Flows](./Flujos). Each step on the canvas is a node.

![A flow on the canvas](/img/intro/flujo.png)

2. If you would rather describe the design and let the app build it, use the **Agent** tab in the [console](/docs/Fundamentos/ProbarElBot).
3. At the bottom of the menu, the lightning bolt shows the training status. **Training pending** is orange: one click trains it. In green it says **AI trained**. Until you train, the bot answers with the previous training.
4. The console, the panel on the right, is the test chat. Write the way the person would.

![The console open to the right of the dashboard](/img/empezando/consola.png)

Every change to intents or entities asks for another training. The lightning bolt turns orange again when something is pending.
