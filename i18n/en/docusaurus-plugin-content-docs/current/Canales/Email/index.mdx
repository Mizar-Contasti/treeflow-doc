# Email (Resend)

Your bot replies to email that arrives at an address of yours. Sending and receiving go through [Resend](https://resend.com). It is a **Stable** channel.

![The Email screen in Channels: inbound webhook and Resend credentials](/img/canales/email.png)

## How it works

1. Someone writes to your address, for example `hello@yourdomain.com`.
2. Resend notifies TreeFlow through a **webhook**.
3. The bot reads the text of the email and replies to the sender, with the subject `Re: …`.

If the bot has replies with blocks (buttons, cards…), the email goes out as **HTML**. Otherwise it goes out as text.

The bot **does not reply to its own address**. That way two automatic replies do not answer each other forever.

## What you need in Resend

An account, a **verified domain**, and inbound email (*Inbound*) for the address the bot will use.

| Field in TreeFlow | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| **Sender email (From Email)** | The bot's address. It must belong to a domain verified in Resend |
| **Sender name (From Name)** | How the sender appears. The default is *Treeflow Assistant* |
| **Resend API Key** | A key with permission to send email |
| **Webhook Signing Secret** | Resend → Webhooks → your webhook → Signing Secret |

The **Signing Secret** lets TreeFlow check that each incoming email is sent by Resend. Without it, TreeFlow does not reply.

## Turn it on

1. Choose your bot in the selector at the top left.
2. Open **Integration → Channels → Email (Resend)**.
3. Copy the **Inbound webhook**. That is the address Resend will notify for each email.
4. In Resend, create a webhook with that URL for incoming email and copy its **Signing Secret**.
5. Fill in sender email, sender name, Resend API Key, and Signing Secret.
6. Click **Save and Integrate Email**. The channel is turned on.

The **ON/OFF** switch at the top turns it off and on without deleting what is saved.

:::note One address, one bot
The bot that replies is the one you had selected when you saved. The same address cannot be registered in two workspaces.
:::

## If it does not reply

- The switch is **ON**.
- The email arrives at the address you put in **From Email**: that is what identifies the bot.
- The **Signing Secret** belongs to the webhook that points at TreeFlow.
- The domain is verified in Resend.
