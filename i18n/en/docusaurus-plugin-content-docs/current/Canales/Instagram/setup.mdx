# Instagram Direct

Connect your bot to the **direct messages** of a professional Instagram account. It is a **Beta** channel.

![The Instagram Direct screen in Channels: the URL for Meta and the account credentials](/img/canales/instagram.png)

## What you need from Meta

A **professional** Instagram account (Business or Creator) and an app in [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/) with access to Instagram messaging.

| Field in TreeFlow | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| **Instagram Account ID** | The ID of the Instagram business account |
| **Verify Token** | You choose it: any text |
| **Instagram Access Token** | A token with the `instagram_manage_messages` permission |
| **App Secret** | Your app → Settings → Basic → App secret |

The **App Secret** lets TreeFlow check that each message really comes from Meta. Without it, TreeFlow does not reply.

## Turn it on

1. Choose your bot in the selector at the top left.
2. Open **Integration → Channels → Instagram Direct**.
3. **Step 1.** Copy the **Callback URL** and paste it in Meta Developers → Instagram → Webhook. In Meta's **Verify token** field, enter the same value you will put in TreeFlow.
4. In Meta, subscribe the webhook to the **messages** field.
5. **Step 2.** Fill in Instagram Account ID, Verify Token, Instagram Access Token, and App Secret.
6. Click **Save and Integrate Instagram**. The channel is turned on.

The **ON/OFF** switch at the top turns it off and on without deleting what is saved.

## What it receives and what it sends

The bot reads text messages, quick replies, and the buttons the person taps.

It replies with **text** (up to 1000 characters). Buttons in the reply appear as **quick replies**, up to 13. Link buttons do not.

## If it does not reply

- The switch is **ON**.
- Meta's **Verify Token** is identical to the one in TreeFlow.
- The **Account ID** is the account that receives the messages.
- The **App Secret** belongs to the same app that sends the messages.
