# WhatsApp

Connect your bot to WhatsApp through the **Meta Cloud API**. It is a **Beta** channel.

![The WhatsApp screen in Channels: status, the URL for Meta, and credentials](/img/canales/whatsapp.png)

## What you need from Meta

An app in [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/) with the **WhatsApp** product and a phone number. These values come from there:

| Field in TreeFlow | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| **Phone Number ID** | The number's identifier, in WhatsApp for your app in Meta |
| **WABA ID** | The ID of your WhatsApp Business account. It is optional |
| **Verify Token** | You choose it: any text |
| **Meta Access Token** | A **permanent** token, generated in Meta Business Manager |
| **App Secret** | Your app → Settings → Basic → App secret |

The **App Secret** is what lets TreeFlow check that each message really comes from Meta. Without it, TreeFlow does not reply.

## Turn it on

1. Choose your bot in the selector at the top left.
2. Open **Integration → Channels → WhatsApp**.
3. **Step 1.** Copy the **Callback URL** and paste it in Meta Developers → WhatsApp → Configuration → Webhook. In Meta's **Verify token** field, enter the same value you will put in TreeFlow.
4. In Meta, subscribe the webhook to the **messages** field.
5. **Step 2.** Fill in Phone Number ID, Verify Token, Meta Access Token, and App Secret (WABA ID is optional).
6. Click **Save and Integrate WhatsApp**. The channel is turned on.

The **ON/OFF** switch at the top turns it off and on without deleting what is saved.

## What the bot sends

Text, and the blocks WhatsApp can draw: **buttons**, **lists**, and files (image, video, document).

:::info Inside the 24-hour window
WhatsApp lets the bot reply freely for 24 hours after the person's last message. After that, an approved Meta template is required, and TreeFlow does not send templates.
:::

## If it does not reply

- The switch is **ON**.
- Meta's **Verify Token** is identical to the one in TreeFlow.
- The **App Secret** belongs to the same app that sends the messages.
- The webhook is subscribed to **messages**.
