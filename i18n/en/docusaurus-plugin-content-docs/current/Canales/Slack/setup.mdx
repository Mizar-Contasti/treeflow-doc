# Slack

Your bot replies in Slack, in channels and in direct messages, with Slack's native blocks. It is a **Stable** channel.

![The Slack screen in Channels: Request URL and app credentials](/img/canales/slack.png)

## What you need in Slack

An app created at [api.slack.com/apps](https://api.slack.com/apps) and installed in your workspace.

| Field in TreeFlow | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| **Bot User OAuth Token** | OAuth & Permissions → *Bot User OAuth Token*. It starts with `xoxb-` |
| **Signing Secret** | Basic Information → App Credentials |

The **Signing Secret** lets TreeFlow check that each event really comes from Slack. Without it, TreeFlow does not reply.

The bot needs the `chat:write` permission so it can reply.

## Turn it on

The URL you paste into Slack is verified with your Signing Secret, so save the credentials in TreeFlow first and then paste the URL into Slack.

1. Choose your bot in the selector at the top left.
2. Open **Integration → Channels → Slack**.
3. Fill in **Bot User OAuth Token** and **Signing Secret**, and click **Save and Integrate Slack**. The channel is turned on.
4. Copy the **Request URL** from that same screen.
5. In your Slack app → **Event Subscriptions**, turn on *Enable Events* and paste the URL into **Request URL**. Slack verifies it right away.
6. Under *Subscribe to bot events*, add `message.channels`, `message.im`, and `app_mention`.
7. For the **buttons** to work, under **Interactivity & Shortcuts** turn on *Interactivity* and paste the same URL.

The **ON/OFF** switch at the top turns it off and on without deleting what is saved.

## What the bot does

- It replies to people's **messages** and to **mentions** (`@tubot`). It ignores messages from bots, edits, and deletions.
- It replies **in the thread** of the message.
- It sends Slack blocks: paragraphs, images, and buttons.
- When someone clicks a button, the bot receives that button's value as if the person had typed it.

## If it does not reply

- The switch is **ON**.
- The **Signing Secret** belongs to the same app that sends the events.
- The bot is **invited to the channel** where you write to it.
- The events from step 6 are subscribed.
