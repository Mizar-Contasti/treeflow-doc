# AI avatars

An **avatar** that speaks the reply, with video generated by [HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com/) or [D-ID](https://www.d-id.com/). It lives on the **Voice** tab of Channels and is a **Beta** channel.

![The AI avatars screen in Channels: provider, API Key, and Avatar ID](/img/canales/avatars.png)

## What you need

An account on HeyGen or D-ID, its **API Key**, and the identifier of the avatar you want to use.

| Field in TreeFlow | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Avatar provider** | HeyGen (Interactive Avatar API) or D-ID |
| **API Key** | Your account key, from the provider's console |
| **Avatar ID / Character ID** | The identifier of the avatar or character to show |

## Turn it on

1. Choose your bot in the selector at the top left.
2. Open **Integration → Channels**, the **Voice** tab, and open **Interactive AI avatars**.
3. Choose the **provider**, and paste the **API Key** and the **Avatar ID**.
4. Click **Save and Integrate Avatars**. The channel is turned on.

The **ON/OFF** switch at the top turns it off and on without deleting what is saved.

## Where you see it

With AI avatars on, the message editor adds an **AI avatars** tab to each reply. It is the same mechanism as [any channel](/docs/Canales/CanalesDeComunicacion#una-respuesta-distinta-por-canal): that is where you write what the avatar will say.

## If it does not work

- The switch is **ON**.
- The **API Key** belongs to the platform you chose as the provider.
- The **Avatar ID** exists on your account.
