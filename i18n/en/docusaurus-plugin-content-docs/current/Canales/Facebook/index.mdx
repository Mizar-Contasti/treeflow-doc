# Facebook Messenger

Connect your bot to Messenger for a **Facebook page**. It is a **Beta** channel.

![The Facebook Messenger screen in Channels: the URL for Meta and the page credentials](/img/canales/facebook.png)

## What you need from Meta

A Facebook page and an app in [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/) with the **Messenger** product.

| Field in TreeFlow | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| **Facebook Page ID** | The numeric ID of your page |
| **Verify Token** | You choose it: any text |
| **Page Access Token** | The page token, with the messaging permission (*messaging*) |
| **App Secret** | Your app → Settings → Basic → App secret |

The **App Secret** lets TreeFlow check that each message really comes from Meta. Without it, TreeFlow does not reply.

## Turn it on

1. Choose your bot in the selector at the top left.
2. Open **Integration → Channels → Facebook Messenger**.
3. **Step 1.** Copy the **Callback URL** and paste it in your Meta app → Messenger → Settings → Webhooks. In Meta's **Verify token** field, enter the same value you will put in TreeFlow.
4. In Meta, subscribe the page to the **messages** and **messaging_postbacks** events.
5. **Step 2.** Fill in Facebook Page ID, Verify Token, Page Access Token, and App Secret.
6. Click **Save and Integrate Facebook**. The channel is turned on.

The **ON/OFF** switch at the top turns it off and on without deleting what is saved.

## What the bot sends

Text, **cards** (generic template, including as a carousel), and **quick replies**.

When someone taps a button or a quick reply, the bot receives what that button carries, not the text shown on it.

:::note One page, one bot
The bot that replies is the one you had selected when you saved. The same Facebook page cannot be registered in two workspaces.
:::

## If it does not reply

- The switch is **ON**.
- Meta's **Verify Token** is identical to the one in TreeFlow.
- The **Page ID** is the page that receives the messages.
- The **App Secret** belongs to the same app that sends the messages.
