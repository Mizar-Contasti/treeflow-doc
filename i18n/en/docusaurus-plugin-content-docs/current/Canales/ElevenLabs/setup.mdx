# ElevenLabs

Turns reply text into **audio** with voices from [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/). It lives on the **Voice** tab of Channels and is a **Stable** channel.

![The ElevenLabs screen in Channels: API Key, voice, and model](/img/canales/elevenlabs.png)

## What you need

An ElevenLabs account and its **API Key**: Profile Settings → API Keys.

| Field in TreeFlow | What it is |
|---|---|
| **ElevenLabs API Key** | Your account key (`xi-api-key`) |
| **Default voice** | The voice used for synthesis. The default is *Rachel* |
| **TTS model ID** | The synthesis model, for example `eleven_multilingual_v2` or `eleven_turbo_v2` |

## Turn it on

1. Choose your bot in the selector at the top left.
2. Open **Integration → Channels**, the **Voice** tab, and open **ElevenLabs Audio AI**.
3. Paste the **API Key**, choose the **voice**, and keep or change the **model**.
4. Click **Save and Integrate ElevenLabs**. The channel is turned on.

The **ON/OFF** switch at the top turns it off and on without deleting what is saved.

## Where you see it

With ElevenLabs on, the message editor adds an **ElevenLabs** tab to each reply. It is the same mechanism as [any channel](/docs/Canales/CanalesDeComunicacion#una-respuesta-distinta-por-canal): that is where you write what will be spoken.

The audio is **MP3**.

## If it does not work

- The switch is **ON**.
- The **API Key** is valid and has credit on ElevenLabs.
- The **model ID** exists on your account.
