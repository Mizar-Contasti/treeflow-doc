# Telegram (Official)

Connect your conversational tree to Telegram to reach millions of users on one of the most popular messaging platforms in the world. TreeFlow has a **native, official** Telegram integration, with no middleware and no third-party bridges.

## Supported features

- **Direct communication**: A native connection through the official Telegram API.
- **Native reply rendering**: The engine's conversational nodes are parsed straight into Telegram formats.

### 14 rich visual blocks (end-to-end support)

TreeFlow automatically turns the visual blocks you set up on your canvas into interactive components in Telegram:

1. **Paragraph**: Plain text messages with Markdown support.
2. **Image**: Native images and photos.
3. **Video**: Native inline video.
4. **Audio**: Voice notes and audio clips.
5. **Files**: Transfer of large documents (PDFs, docx, and so on).
6. **Inline buttons**: Action buttons embedded under the messages.
7. **Quick replies**: Custom keyboards at the bottom of the chat.
8. **Accordion**: Logical grouping of long text (compound text parsing).
9. **Dropdown**: Parsed into multi-select button sets.
10. **Share location**: Static maps (latitude/longitude).
11. **Request location**: A special button that reads the GPS location from the user's phone.
12. **Divider**: Visual separators.
13. **Card**: A compound message with a title, a subtitle, and buttons.
14. **Carousel**: A horizontal list of cards (optimized sequential rendering).

:::tip Automated tests
The Telegram integration is backed by a solid end-to-end test suite (with Playwright). All 14 rich blocks work with exact parity with the web version.
:::

## Setup and activation

It is the shortest channel to turn on: there is no URL to copy and no app to create, only a token.

![The Telegram screen: the BotFather steps, the token field, and the switch](/img/canales/telegram.png)

1. Choose your bot in the selector at the top left.
2. In Telegram, open **@BotFather** and send `/newbot`. Follow the instructions to give it a name and a *username*.
3. Copy the **HTTP API Token** BotFather gives you. It looks like this: `123456789:ABC…`
4. In TreeFlow, open **Integration → Channels → Telegram**.
5. Paste the token into **Telegram Bot Token** and click **Save and Connect Telegram**.

When you save, TreeFlow registers the webhook with Telegram on its own and the channel is **turned on**. Write to your bot on Telegram to try it.

The **ON/OFF** switch at the top turns it off and on without deleting the token.
