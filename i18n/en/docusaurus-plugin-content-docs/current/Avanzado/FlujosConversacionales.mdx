# Flows

A flow is the drawing of the conversation: nodes and arrows. The tree can have several. One is marked **Primary** and is the one that starts if the first button in the console says **Default**.

In Cafetería the primary one is **flujo_principal**. **Restaurante** is the map of the place. The screen, button by button, is in [Flows](/docs/Fundamentos/Flujos). This page walks through that map.

## The list

**Flows** opens the cards. You search by name. **New flow** creates an empty one. The copy icon duplicates it. The trash deletes it, except the primary one. **Templates** applies a business type: intents, entities, and the drawing. Applying it again does not overwrite the question of a parameter that already existed.

![flujo_principal marked Primary, and the Restaurante flow](/img/flujos/lista.png)

## Settings

When you open a flow there are two tabs: **Design** and **Settings**.

![Name, description, and the primary flow switch](/img/flujos/configuracion.png)

- **Flow name.** This is the identifier. In the console it shows on the first button.
- **Description.** So you remember what it is for. The person does not see it.
- **Primary flow.** There can be only one. The one that already is primary cannot be turned off from here: you turn it on in the other flow and save. The primary flow cannot be deleted. It is the starting point when the console says **Default**.

At the top right, the save icon turns on when something is unsaved: a moved node, an arrow, the name, the switch. Closing the editor does not save on its own. Moving a node does not change what the bot replies. Changing the intent, a pattern, or an entity does: the lightning bolt in the menu turns orange again and you have to train.

## The canvas

**Design** is the map. Each node can be dragged. At the bottom left are zoom, center, and **Tidy Up**, which arranges the nodes.

![Start, the restaurant intents, the event, and the jump to flujo_principal](/img/flujos/lienzo.png)

The orange **+** creates the node according to where you start from. From a context it offers an intent or an event. From an intent or an event it offers a context. The brown arrow, under the +, is dragged to another node to connect them. The solid line is the path. The dotted line is an event.

| Node | What it is |
|---|---|
| Context, green | Where the bot waits. In Restaurante it is named **Inicio** |
| Intent, orange | It fires when the engine recognizes that intent |
| Event, the lightning bolt | It did not understand (`sys.no-match`) or nothing arrived (`sys.no-input`) |

The `flujo_principal` pill is a link: from this drawing you can also jump to that flow.

## Where it waits

The context does not say a phrase. It lists what can be recognized while the conversation is there.

![Inicio, with the intents and the event for that stretch](/img/flujos/contexto.png)

- **Intents.** The ones for this stretch. You pick a node that already exists, or you create one. Removing the link does not delete the intent.
- **Events.** The one for not understanding, the one for silence, or a name that another step fires.
- **Linked flows.** From here the other flow's intents count too. If both have `saludo`, while you are in one of them the node from that flow runs, not the one from the other.

The primary flow links to Restaurante. That is why, starting on **Default**, you can also order or book. The greeting that replies is the one from the flow you are in.

## A step, and back

The orange node does not train the intent. It points at it. Patterns and parameters are edited in [Intents](/docs/Fundamentos/Intenciones).

![saludo: the intent, the message, and the return to Inicio](/img/flujos/conversacion.png)

**Conversation**

- **Intent.** If it is already in this flow, or in a linked one, the picker marks it **In use**.
- **Response.** A [message](/docs/Fundamentos/Mensajes) template, **+ Create template**, or **Custom message**. It is sent once the data is in, after this node's tools.
- **Transition.** Where it goes next:
  - **None.**
  - A context on the canvas, or a new one.
  - **Switch flow**, to the start of another one.
  - **Restart current flow.**
  - **Transfer to human.** Hands the conversation to the [help desk](./MesaDeAyuda).

`saludo`, the hours, the menu, and the order go back to **Inicio**. That is why the next phrase is heard again.

## Functionality {#funcionalidad}

Until an intent is chosen, this tab asks you to go back to Conversation.

![Scripts, tools, end the capture, and the webhook](/img/flujos/funcionalidad.png)

- **Scripts** and **tools.** What this step calls outside. They are created in [Tools](/docs/Herramientas).
- **Ends a capture in progress.** If the person says this while the bot is asking for data. On, the question is treated as finished: cancel or say goodbye. Off, which is the default, the bot replies and picks the question back up, with what was already written.
- **Call webhook on this node.** The tree's webhook runs when passing through here, before the tools. If there is no active one, nothing happens.

The context has no response and no "ends a capture". The event does not close the capture either: that belongs to the intent.

## If it does not recognize the phrase

![sys.no-match and its message](/img/flujos/evento.png)

The lightning bolt is chosen or typed. The ones the engine fires on its own are `sys.no-match` and `sys.no-input`. A name of your own is fired by another step; if it is already on another event in the flow, it is marked **In use**.

In Restaurante the message is "Sorry, I didn't understand that. Could you say it again?". The transition is the same list as on the intent. The usual choice is to return to the context.

In that message, and in the node's tools and scripts, the phrase that was not understood is in **`{$last_no_match}`**: `No entendí "{$last_no_match}", ¿puedes decirlo de otra forma?`. Nothing has to be turned on. See [System variables](/docs/Avanzado/Funciones#variables-del-sistema).

## When they ask for a person

`hablar_con_asesor` does not return to Inicio. The transition is **Transfer to a human agent**.

![hablar_con_asesor: the message and the help desk](/img/diseno/asesor.png)

It warns first: "I'll connect you with someone on the team". **Ends a capture in progress** is on: if they say it in the middle of an order, it stops asking and hands the conversation over.
